About Sri Aadhya

Healthcare with a patient-first approach.

Sri Aadhya Multi Speciality Hospital is a multi-speciality hospital in Paravada, Visakhapatnam, located opposite Econa Hotel. We care for patients across orthopaedics, spine, hand surgery and podiatric care, with a calm, clean environment and clear communication at every step.

The hospital is open 24 hours, every day — call us or visit us directly whenever you need care.
